Orrery

The Solar System's minor planets, shown in the order they were discovered. Each dot is an asteroid on its orbit. New discoveries appear green and fade to grey; the date and running count are at the bottom left.

Use the [⏸︎] / [▶] control or the date at the bottom left to control the timeline. Open [+] options (top left) to change the speed, switch between the 2D and 3D renderers or raise the resolution. Negative speed rewinds. Scroll to zoom; in 3D, drag to orbit the camera.

Orbits are fixed ellipses from Minor Planet Center orbital elements, so positions are approximate. Only numbered minor planets with a known discovery date are included.

Data: Minor Planet Center via orrery-data. Code: GitHub.
